Q1. How has the distribution of content ratings changed over time?

Answer:
Over time, Netflix has increasingly focused on mature content. Ratings such as TV-MA and TV-14 dominate recent years, indicating a strategic shift toward adult audiences. Family and kids’ content exists but represents a smaller portion of the library.

Q2. Is there a relationship between content age and its type?

Answer:
Yes. Movies are often added in the same year as their release, reflecting Netflix Originals. TV shows tend to have higher age variance, as older series are licensed and added later. This shows Netflix balances new releases with classic acquisitions.

Q3. Release year vs year added – trends?

Answer:
A strong spike at zero-year difference suggests Netflix prioritizes original content. However, a long tail indicates heavy investment in older licensed content to maintain a diverse and deep catalog.

Q4. Most common word pairs/themes in descriptions?

Answer:
Common themes include life, family, love, friendship, discovery, and journey. This indicates Netflix content heavily focuses on emotional and relationship-driven storytelling.

Q5. Who are the top directors on Netflix?

Answer:
Directors from the US and India dominate the platform. However, a significant number of entries list the director as “Unknown,” highlighting incomplete metadata in publicly available datasets.
